# Product

## Register

brand

The `/demo` area is a product surface (app UI for the proposed Arihant Business System, marketplace and WhatsApp assistant). Treat any task under `/demo` as register `product`.

## Users

Purchase managers and owners at plastic processing units (injection moulders, film and raffia extruders, blow moulders, pipe makers) and traders across India, mostly in Maharashtra and Gujarat. They usually arrive with a grade in mind ("MI3530", "Reliance raffia", "GAIL LLDPE") and check the site on a phone between machine checks in a bright factory office. They need to see quickly that Arihant stocks the grade and is a real, established stockist, then send a rate request or call.

## Product Purpose

Arihant Polymer is a Mumbai (Malad West) partnership that has traded and distributed polymer granules since 1991: PP, HDPE, LLDPE, LDPE, polystyrene, PVC, PET, ABS and more from Reliance, GAIL, IOCL, HMEL, OPaL, Haldia and international producers. Today it relies on phone calls and an IndiaMART catalogue page. The site replaces that page with its own catalogue in Arihant's name, so buyers searching a grade find Arihant and send a qualified rate request (grade, quantity, delivery point) instead of a vague enquiry. Success: rate requests with a grade and quantity, and calls.

## Brand Personality

Dependable, quick, no-nonsense. The voice is a seasoned trader's stock sheet and the print on a polymer bag: grade codes, producers, process, quantities. Short sentences, facts with units, no adjectives doing the selling. Visitors should leave thinking "they have it, they've done this for 35 years, and they'll give me a straight rate".

## Anti-references

- IndiaMART catalogue pages: endless identical product tiles, "Get Best Quote" on everything, placeholder prices like Rs 100/kg.
- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient hero, big stat numbers, icon card grids, glass cards.
- Heavy corporate or chemical-giant sites: refinery stock photos, navy and grey, "global solutions" copy.
- Earlier Onshore Labs sites (Star Asiya, Bombino, CEO Rules): no shared layouts, palettes or type.

## Design Principles

1. The grade code is the hero. Buyers think in codes and producers; show them big and make them searchable.
2. One step from a rate. Every page and every grade leads to a rate request carrying the grade.
3. Built for daylight and one hand. High contrast, large targets, works on a phone in a bright office.
4. Only what is true. Facts come from Arihant's own listing; anything unconfirmed is marked or left out. No invented prices.
5. A stockist, not a portal. The site should feel like a trader's register and a bag of material, not a marketplace template.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (all motion degrades to static). Touch targets at least 44px. Every interactive component keyboard operable with visible focus. Plain English for readers whose first language may be Hindi, Gujarati or Marathi; grade codes and numbers do most of the work.
